# Salary Bands and Compensation Policy

All offers must fall inside the approved band for the role level. Amounts are annual CTC in INR. Variable pay (up to 10% of CTC) is part of the CTC for levels L3 and above.

Engineering levels (Backend, Frontend, Full Stack, Mobile, DevOps, QA Automation):
- L1 Software Engineer (0-1 years): 6,00,000 - 9,00,000
- L2 Software Engineer (1-3 years): 9,00,000 - 16,00,000
- L3 Senior Software Engineer (3-6 years): 16,00,000 - 28,00,000
- L4 Staff / Lead Engineer (6-10 years): 28,00,000 - 45,00,000
- L5 Principal Engineer / Engineering Manager (10+ years): 45,00,000 - 70,00,000

Full Stack roles use the same band as Backend roles at the same level.
DevOps / SRE roles get a 10% premium on the band upper limit.

Data Science and ML Engineering:
- Data Analyst (0-2 years): 6,00,000 - 11,00,000
- Data Scientist / ML Engineer (2-5 years): 14,00,000 - 26,00,000
- Senior Data Scientist / Senior ML Engineer (5+ years): 26,00,000 - 42,00,000

Product and Design:
- Associate Product Manager (0-2 years): 10,00,000 - 16,00,000
- Product Manager (2-6 years): 18,00,000 - 35,00,000
- Product Designer (2-5 years): 12,00,000 - 24,00,000

Business roles:
- Sales Development Representative (0-2 years): 4,50,000 - 7,00,000 + incentives
- Account Executive (2-5 years): 9,00,000 - 18,00,000 + incentives
- HR / Talent Acquisition Specialist (1-4 years): 5,00,000 - 10,00,000
- Customer Success Manager (2-5 years): 8,00,000 - 15,00,000

Interns: stipend 30,000 - 50,000 per month (engineering), 20,000 - 30,000 (non-engineering).

Offer rules:
- Start offers at or below the band midpoint unless interview performance is "strong hire".
- Anything above the band requires VP approval and cannot be generated by the AI system.
- Joining bonus up to 1,00,000 may be offered to buy out notice periods (HR approval required).
