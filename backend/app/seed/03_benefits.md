# Benefits, Leave and Perks

Health and insurance:
- Group medical insurance of 5,00,000 INR covering employee, spouse, children and parents.
- Term life insurance of 3x annual CTC and accidental cover.
- Free annual health check-up and online doctor consultations.

Leave policy:
- 24 days of paid leave per year plus 10 public holidays.
- 12 sick days per year.
- 26 weeks maternity leave, 4 weeks paternity / adoption leave.
- Wellness week: the company shuts down for the last week of December.

Work and growth:
- Hybrid work: 3 days in office, 2 days remote for most roles.
- Learning budget of 50,000 INR per year for courses, certifications and conferences.
- Latest MacBook Pro or equivalent laptop plus home-office allowance of 15,000 INR.
- ESOPs for L3 and above, vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff.

Other perks:
- Free lunch and snacks in office, cab facility after 8 PM.
- Monthly internet reimbursement of 1,500 INR.
- Quarterly team outings and an annual offsite.
