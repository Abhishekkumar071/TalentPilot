# Hiring Policy and Job Description Guidelines

Job description rules:
- Use inclusive, gender-neutral language ("you", "they"). Avoid words like "rockstar", "ninja", "young", "energetic", "native speaker".
- Never state age, gender, marital status, religion, caste or college-tier requirements.
- A degree is "preferred", not mandatory, for engineering roles; equivalent practical experience is accepted.
- Every JD must show the salary range from the approved salary band.
- List at most 8 must-have skills; keep requirements realistic for the level.
- Mention hybrid work policy and benefits.

Standard hiring process for all roles:
1. Resume screening (AI-assisted, reviewed by humans)
2. Online assessment (40-45 minutes, proctored)
3. AI-led first-round interview (about 20 minutes)
4. Final conversation with the hiring manager
5. Offer and background verification

Fairness rules for screening:
- Identity details (name, photo, gender, age) are hidden from AI scoring.
- AI never auto-rejects for proctoring flags; flags are reviewed by a human.
- Every final hiring decision and every offer must be approved by a human recruiter.

Offer and probation:
- Probation period is 6 months for all full-time employees; notice period during probation is 15 days, after confirmation 60 days.
- Offers are valid for 5 days from issue.
- Default joining date is 30 days after offer acceptance; earlier joining is welcome.
- Background verification covers employment history and education.

Replanning rule: if a posting gets fewer qualified applicants than needed, the recruiting team may broaden the role title (for example Backend Engineer to Full Stack Engineer), relax non-essential skills, widen the experience range by up to 1 year, or open the role to remote candidates in India. The salary must remain within the band of the target level.
