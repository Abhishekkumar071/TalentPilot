# Company Overview - {{COMPANY}}

{{COMPANY}} is a product-based software company headquartered in Noida, India, with a second office in Bengaluru. Founded in 2016, we build cloud software for logistics and supply-chain companies. Our flagship product, NimbusFlow, helps 400+ businesses track shipments, optimise delivery routes and automate warehouse operations.

We are a team of about 320 people across Engineering, Product, Design, Data Science, Sales, Customer Success and People Operations. We are profitable, Series-B funded, and growing at 60% year over year.

Mission: make every shipment in India predictable, efficient and green.

Culture and values:
- Ownership: we trust people with real problems end to end.
- Learn in public: weekly demo days, blameless post-mortems, internal tech talks.
- Customer obsession: every engineer spends one day per quarter with customer success.
- Inclusion: we hire for skills and potential, not pedigree. College tier is never a hiring criterion.

Default office for new roles is Noida (Sector 62). Bengaluru (HSR Layout) is available for Engineering and Data roles.
